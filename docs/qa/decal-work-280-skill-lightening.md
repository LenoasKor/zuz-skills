# Decal WORK-280: portable skill improvements

This is the source portion of the owner's existing Decal WORK-280, not a new local ticket or authority to modify unrelated consumers. Two independent planning reviews requested compatibility-first consolidation, evidence-bound handoffs, conditional loading, and explicit separation of static contracts from model behavior evaluation.

## Changes

- Preserve all 31 IDs and four modules. Orient and status-summary retain first-look/resume entry points but avoid repeating full exploration. A quick risk review remains cheap and does not require an independent worker.
- Handoff carries root/branch/HEAD/dirty state, verification commands and real results, running Build ID mismatch, failed attempts and next owner/action. Approval records are context, not new permission.
- Risk review and health diagnosis distinguish observations, assumptions and unverified coverage instead of unconditional success or Task-document-only evidence.
- Motion implementation/audit/review keep separate authority. Remove forced finding/plan quotas; no findings create no work. Reduced motion can use static/fade results. Duration defaults and documented exceptions agree across entry/companions.
- Generic slice planning/hygiene no longer impose a specific Decal historical slice number or App.tsx shape.
- Adapted AUDIT/STANDARDS are labeled adapted in vendor provenance; original upstream bytes, commit and license remain unchanged.

## Scope decisions

A deletion quota would be harmful. First-look vs resume, quick diff review vs project-wide diagnosis, four report decision purposes, and generic vs Showcase handoff retain distinct value. This pass removes redundant guidance/forced steps, not their discoverable IDs. No use-frequency data was collected.

Version 3.14.1 is a compatible prompt-quality patch. No existing immutable contract bytes, installer permissions, module selection, or provider paths change. Published availability and consumer rollout require separate verified receipts; this source note alone is not a deployment claim.
